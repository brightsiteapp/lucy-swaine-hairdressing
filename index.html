Welcome to Meraki Salon

Hair, brows and beauty — done properly

A friendly, fully qualified team of hairdressers and beauty specialists working from a bright, modern salon on Hull Road in Hessle, a few minutes from Hull and the Humber Bridge.

Every appointment starts with a proper chat about what you want and what will actually work for you — whether that’s your hair, your brows or your lashes. Hair colour services include a free 15-minute consultation, and new colour clients have a patch test at least 48 hours beforehand.
