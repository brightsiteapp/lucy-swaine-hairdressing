Welcome to Lucy Swaine Hairdressing

Colour, cutting and extensions — done properly

A friendly, fully qualified hairdresser and hair extensionist working from a bright, modern salon on Hull Road in Hessle, a few minutes from Hull and the Humber Bridge.

Every appointment starts with a proper chat about what you want and what will actually work with your hair — your colour history, your condition, and how much upkeep you want to take on. Colour services include a free 15-minute consultation, and new colour clients have a patch test at least 48 hours beforehand.
